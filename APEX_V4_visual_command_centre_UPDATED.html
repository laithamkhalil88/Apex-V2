Starting strategy candidates

APEX ranks alternatives using measured compound wear from your sessions, track-specific learning, temperature signals when available, stint pace and pit-loss estimates. Real-world F1 history is contextual evidence only: it is not interchangeable with F1 game physics. External browser research is not automatically connected to this offline page.
